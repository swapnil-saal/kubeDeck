# Appearance

KubeDeck has a light mode and a dark mode, and several colour palettes for each. You choose one palette for dark and one for light, so switching modes always lands on your two favourites. Change them in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Mode

Under **Mode**, pick:

| Mode | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Always light |
| **Dark** | Always dark. This is the default |
| **System** | Follow your operating system. KubeDeck shows which mode your system is in right now |

The sun/moon button in the header switches between light and dark in one click.

## Palettes

Each palette card shows a small preview of KubeDeck in its colours. The palette under **Dark palette** is used whenever KubeDeck is dark; the one under **Light palette** whenever it is light.

| Dark palettes | | Light palettes | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OLED black** (default) | True black, brightest text | **Paper** (default) | Clean white, brand teal |
| **High contrast** | Black, white text, bright borders | **High contrast** | Black on white, strong borders |
| **Charcoal** | Neutral charcoal, brand cyan | **Arctic** | Cool blue-white, blue |
| **Midnight** | Deep navy, sky blue | **Sand** | Warm paper, terracotta |
| **Forest** | Green-black, emerald | **Meadow** | Soft green, deep green |
| **Plum** | Aubergine, violet | | |

A palette applies as soon as you select it. Other open KubeDeck tabs pick up the change too.

## Where it is saved

Mode and palettes are saved in this browser (or in the desktop app) only, not on the server. On a shared web KubeDeck, each person keeps their own choice, and it does not follow you to another browser or computer.

The documentation at `/docs` on the same KubeDeck opens in the light or dark mode you chose.

## Motion

Animations follow your operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
